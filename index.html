It started young.

Most boys outgrow it. Steve never did. From the very first time one was placed in his tiny hands, he knew. This was the thing.

While other men chase wealth and status, Steve chases something purer. He has held balls of every size. He has cradled the small ones with care and wrestled the truly massive ones to the ground.

He doesn't discriminate. Smooth or textured, firm or soft, a tidy little pair or one absolute unit. If it's round, Steve wants to hold it.
